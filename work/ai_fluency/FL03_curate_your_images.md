## 2. The Keepers vs. Rejections (Where Real Captures Beat AI)

### A. About Section (Personal Photo)
* **Chosen Asset:** Real headshot photo (`screenshots/profile_photo.png`).
* **Why Real:** Technical founders and engineering leads hire humans, not AI avatars. An AI-generated professional portrait undermines authenticity immediately.
* **Public Repository Privacy Note:** *In this public GitHub repository, a minimalist avatar image (`screenshots/profile_photo.png`) is used in place of an actual photographic portrait strictly due to personal privacy constraints. For the live deployed portfolio and client interactions, a real photographic portrait is used to preserve trust and authentic human attribution.

### B. Case Study 1: Distributed LLM Inference Cluster
* **Chosen Asset:** A clean architectural diagram of the two-node Ethernet connection and layer sharding via `exo`.
* **Rejected AI Asset:** A hyper-realistic 3D glowing "neural network brain" floating over a server rack.
* **Discernment Note (Why Rejected):** The 3D render is generic visual noise. It conveys zero technical information about VRAM allocation, Ethernet transport, or inference latency trade-offs.

### C. Case Study 2: Real-Time Edge Vision & Navigation System
* **Chosen Asset:** A direct, clean screenshot of the live camera feed showing YOLOv8 bounding boxes, directional collision zones, and the 30+ FPS counter.
* **Rejected AI Asset:** A futuristic graphic of a robotic eye scanning an urban grid with stylized holographic HUD rings.
* **Discernment Note (Why Rejected):** A Lead Perception Engineer evaluates frame rate stability and spatial bounding logic. A stylized HUD graphic is stock marketing art that hides whether the model actually runs.

### D. Case Study 3: Search Performance Opportunity Engine
* **Chosen Asset:** A clean multi-bar plot comparing the client-holdout F1-score (+20.3% lift) against the heuristic baseline.
* **Rejected AI Asset:** A glowing 3D growth graph with floating Google/search icons.
* **Discernment Note (Why Rejected):** The technical proof of this capstone relies on leak-free validation metrics and data contracts, which only an empirical benchmark plot demonstrates.

## 3. Connective Tissue & Consistency Rules

* **Palette Uniformity:** Any auxiliary icons or structural diagram lines strictly adhere to the Identity Kit tokens (`#111827` primary, `#E5E7EB` borders, and `#0284C7` accent).
* **Frame, Never Upstage:** Every image has a 1px neutral border (`#E5E7EB`) and generous padding to ensure the technical receipts remain the focal point.
* **No Unearned Flair:** Zero decorative stock illustrations, floating glitter, or faux-holographic elements.
